Server: PostgreSQL 18.0. 17 tables, 1 view, 45 indexes.

Why this file exists. Until this capture, no complete record of this database
existed anywhere outside the server. schema.sql is a pg_dump from 2025-10-23
covering 7 tables, at least one of them wrongly (see its header). The migrations
in backend/ create 2 more and otherwise only ADD COLUMN to tables they assume
exist. Eight tables and the dataset_id column that scopes every cohort appeared
in no SQL in this repository at all.

backend/migrate_kb_base_tables.sql was transcribed from what is below, and
backend/tests/test_schema_coverage.py checks the transcription has not drifted.

Not captured here, and therefore still not reproducible from git:
`\d hpc_dictionary`, `\d hpc_malignant_details`, `\d hpc_non_malignant_details`,
`\d hpc_survival_analysis`, and `\d+ slide_metadata` (the view's definition).
Re-run those and append them below.

Note two mismatches visible here, both recorded as found:
  * the UNIQUE constraint is on (samples, slides) and does NOT include
    dataset_id, so two cohorts holding the same slide id cannot both have a
    summary row;
  * hpl_profile_summary.slides is varchar(150) while the proportion table's
    slides — the referencing side of the foreign key — is varchar(170).

================================================================================
Same columns as tile_hpc_heatmap.

The two primary-key constraint names are transposed relative to their tables:
tile_hpc_heatmap's is hpc_heatmap_pkey, tile_hpc_heatmap_old's is
tile_hpc_heatmap_pkey. That is what renaming the live table under a running
system leaves behind. Recorded as found.
